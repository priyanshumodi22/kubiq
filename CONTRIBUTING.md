# Contributing to kubiq

Thanks for helping improve kubiq. By submitting a contribution, you confirm
that you have the right to submit it and agree that your contribution is
licensed under the Elastic License 2.0 (ELv2) as part of kubiq.

Keep pull requests focused, include tests when practical, and do not include
secrets, third-party code, or assets unless you have the right to contribute
them.

Contributions do not grant a right to use the kubiq name, logos, or other brand
assets. See [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).
